    Brewing Better Coffee, Vol. 1

    Stop following recipes. Start building them. A 90-minute, small-group workshop at the roastery, built on side-by-side brewing experiments. No advanced coffee knowledge needed. Come curious.

    Length
    90 minutes, Saturdays 4:30 to 6 pm
    Group size
    Up to 10 people
    Price
    $99 + HST per seat
    You take home
    100 g each of the two workshop coffees
    1. Strength and extractionOn the V60, we change grind, ratio and bypass water to taste the difference between how much we extract and how strong the cup is. Brewed with Ethiopia Kokose.
    2. Water and flavourSame coffee, same method, different water, to show how much water composition changes what is in the cup. Brewed with Wilton Benitez Geisha.
